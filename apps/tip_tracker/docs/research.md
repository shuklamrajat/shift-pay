# What tip tracker users like and dislike (23 Nov 2026)

Reddit threads did not come up in web search from here (Reddit pages are not indexed by the search tool this session uses, and site filters were refused), so the user voice below comes from public App Store and Play reviews and review aggregators. Counts are approximate and the samples are small.

## What people value
- Totals and averages by day, week, month and year (most praised, about half of 20 ServerLife reviews).
- Simple, fast entry (about 8 of 20).
- Real hourly rate per shift (about 5 of 20; also TipSee, Tip Bucket).
- Long history that's never lost (about 5 of 20).
- Multiple jobs, compared side by side (TipSee: "a single place where I can compare how much money I'm making at various jobs").
- Cash vs card tips tracked apart (Tip Tracker°).
- Notes per shift (drawer counts, who left early).
- One-time fair price; free tier enough for basics.

## What makes them angry
- **Losing data.** Updates that wiped years of history (Just the Tips 2017, Tip Bucket 2017), login lockouts after updates (ServerLife, TipSee 2022, Just the Tips 2024), "new account, your data will not load" (ServerLife help page), emailed backups that never worked (TipSee), server errors ("Why must I be dependent on your janky and questionable servers?").
- **Updates that silently change the math** (Tip Bucket take-home change).
- **Forced cash + card entry** when they only have one kind (ServerLife).
- **Hourly-only shifts need a $0.01 tip to count** (Just the Tips).
- **Paywall and ad pop-ups** while entering (ServerLife, TipSee).
- **Views that jump** to Settings or back to the current month (ServerLife, Just the Tips).
- **Can't remove an old job without deleting its history** (Tip Tracker°).
- **Shifts over 24 h** miscalculated and locked (Tip Tracker°).
- Reminders that never arrive; no tutorial on first run.

## Requests
- Automatic tip-out calculation; tip-out rules (% of sales, flat).
- Average by day of the week.
- Reconcile card tips against the paycheck.
- Local or own-cloud backup with clear data ownership.
- Hourly-only shifts without workarounds.
