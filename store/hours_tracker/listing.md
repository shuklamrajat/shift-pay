## Full description

```text
Know your pay before payday. Wagebird is a friendly hours tracker and overtime calculator that turns your shifts into the paycheck you should expect, so checking your pay stub takes seconds, not a spreadsheet.

Tap to clock in, tap to clock out, and Wagebird works out regular hours, overtime and double time with the rules your job actually uses. No account, no ads, and your hours stay on your phone.

✨ HOW IT WORKS
1. Add your job: its name, hourly rate and overtime rules
2. Clock in when you start and clock out when you finish
3. Open Pay to see what you've earned this week, or for any dates you choose

⏱️ LOG SHIFTS YOUR WAY
• Clock in and out with one tap, or add a shift afterwards
• Overnight shifts that cross midnight are counted as one shift
• Unpaid breaks are taken off automatically
• Forgot to clock out? Fix the end time later
• Overlapping shifts are caught before they're saved
• Set a different hourly rate for a single shift when the job pays extra

💰 OVERTIME, WORKED OUT RIGHT
• Daily overtime, for example after 8 hours in a day
• Weekly overtime, for example after 40 regular hours in a week
• Daily double time, for example after 12 hours in a day
• Your own multipliers: time and a half, double time, or whatever your contract says
• Choose the day your work week starts and the time your workday starts, so night shifts land on the right day
• Daylight saving nights are handled, so a 9-hour night shift counts as 9 hours

🧾 SHOWS ITS WORKING
Every total splits into regular, overtime and double-time hours, each with its own pay, so when a number on your payslip looks off you can see exactly where the difference is. A clear pay split bar shows how much of your week came from overtime.

📊 SEE YOUR WEEK AT A GLANCE
• This week's hours and pay, front and centre
• A day-by-day chart that shows regular time and overtime
• Pay totals for any dates you pick: regular, overtime and double-time hours and pay
• Change your rate and your past shifts keep the rate they were worked at

🐦 MEET WAGEBIRD
Wagebird is designed to feel good to use: a little bird that keeps your timesheet honest, smooth animations, charts that grow as your week fills up, and a calm dark mode for night shifts. Prefer less movement? Wagebird follows your phone's reduce-motion setting.

📄 TIMESHEETS FOR PAYROLL (PRO)
• Export a PDF timesheet for your manager, payroll or your own records
• Export CSV for Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers
• Track more than one job, each with its own hourly rate and overtime rules

🔒 PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account and no sign-up
• No ads and no tracking
• Your shifts are stored on your phone and never uploaded by us
• Exports only go where you choose to send them

❓ GOOD TO KNOW
• Works offline: no internet needed to log shifts or see your pay
• Pick your currency in Settings
• Light or dark theme, or follow your phone
• Built for hourly pay, including part-time, casual and gig work

WHO IT'S FOR
Hourly workers who want to check their pay: nurses and carers on 12-hour shifts, retail and restaurant staff, warehouse and factory teams, security, drivers, contractors and anyone working more than one job. If you've ever wondered whether your overtime was paid right, Wagebird gives you the numbers to check.

FREE AND PRO
Wagebird is free for one job with unlimited shifts, overtime rules and pay totals. Wagebird Pro adds timesheet export and more jobs: $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once for life. Prices may vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy.

Wagebird is a calculator to help you check your pay. Overtime law differs by country, state and contract, so confirm the rules that apply to you with your employer or payslip.

Questions or ideas? We read every message, and the best ideas end up in the app.
```
