## Full description (4000 max)

Know exactly what your next paycheck should be.

Shift Pay logs your hours and works out regular time, overtime and double
time with the rules your job actually uses, so you can check your pay stub
in seconds.

LOG SHIFTS YOUR WAY
• Clock in and out with one tap, or type hours in afterwards
• Overnight shifts, split shifts and unpaid breaks
• Forgot to clock out? Edit the end time later
• Overlapping shifts are caught before they're saved

OVERTIME DONE RIGHT
• Daily limit (for example over 8 hours) and weekly limit (over 40 hours)
• Your own multipliers: time and a half, double time
• Pick the day your work week starts
• Handles daylight saving nights correctly, so a 9-hour night shift counts
  as 9 hours

SEE YOUR PAY
• This week's hours and pay at a glance
• Totals for any date range: regular, overtime and double-time hours
• Pay rate changes don't rewrite your past shifts

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No account, no sign-up
• Your hours stay on your phone

SHIFT PAY PRO
• Export timesheets as CSV for spreadsheets and PDF for your manager or payroll
• Track more than one job, each with its own rate and overtime rules
• $19.99 a year with a 7-day free trial, or $39.99 once

Shift Pay is a calculator to help you check your pay. Overtime law differs
by country, state and contract, so confirm the rules that apply to you.
