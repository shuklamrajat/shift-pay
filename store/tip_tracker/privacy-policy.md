# Tipwell privacy policy

_Draft for the Tipwell page on https://shuklamrajat.github.io/widdle-privacy/
(source: the widdle-privacy repo). Not published yet; the published page is
the one that counts. Last updated: [date of publishing]._

**Short version: what you enter stays on your phone. We don't collect it,
see it or sell it. Tipwell shows one Google AdMob banner ad, which collects
some device data to show and measure ads.**

## What Tipwell stores
Your jobs, shifts, hours, tips, sales, tip-out and notes are saved only on
your device, inside the app's own storage. There is no account and no cloud
sync. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Backups
Tipwell saves a backup file on your device each day you open it and keeps the
last 14. When you choose to save a backup or export a report, the app writes
the file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from
there (Google Drive, email, files) is your choice; we never receive it.

## What we collect
Nothing ourselves. Tipwell contains no analytics or tracking code of ours,
and it doesn't send your entries anywhere.

## Ads
Tipwell shows a Google AdMob banner. AdMob collects and shares with Google the
advertising ID and other device IDs, IP address (approximate location), ad
interactions and diagnostics, encrypted in transit, to show and measure ads
and prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for consent
first and the answer can be changed in Settings, Ad privacy choices.

## Purchases
Tipwell has no purchases at the moment. If upgrades are added later, they
will be paid through Google Play or the App Store and this policy will be
updated first.

## Children
Tipwell is for a general audience and is not directed at children.
