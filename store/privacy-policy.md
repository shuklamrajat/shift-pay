# Privacy policy

_Applies to Wagebird, Wrenchnote and Housetally.
Last updated: [date of publishing]._

**Short version: your data stays on your phone. We don't collect it, see it
or sell it.**

## What the apps store
Everything you enter (shifts and pay rates, vehicles and fill-ups, items,
photos and receipts) is saved only on your device, inside the app's own
storage. There is no account and no cloud sync. Uninstalling the app deletes
this data.

## What we collect
Nothing. The apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and
they don't send your entries anywhere.

## Camera and photos
Housetally asks for the camera or photo library only when you add a
picture to an item. The picture is copied into the app's storage on your
device and is not uploaded.

## Exports
When you export a CSV or PDF, the app writes the file on your device and
opens your phone's share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice.

## Purchases
Upgrades are paid through Google Play or the App Store. We use [RevenueCat]
to check whether you have Pro; it receives a random app user id and your
purchase receipts from the store, not your entries. See RevenueCat's privacy
policy for details.

## Ratings
The apps may ask, rarely, whether you'd like to rate them, using the store's
own rating dialog.

## Children
The apps are for a general audience and are not directed at children.
