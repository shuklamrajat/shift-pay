# Store names for the first three apps

Researched 2026-11-12 by web search across the App Store, Google Play listings and app directories
(appbrain, appgoblin, mwm.ai, Product Hunt, alternativeto). Direct store search and the App Store
search API were not reachable from here, so "no app found" means none showed up in web search, not a
guaranteed clean store. No trademark database was searched. Before committing, type each brand into the
Play Store and App Store search boxes and run a free search on USPTO (tmsearch.uspto.gov) and WIPO
Global Brand Database.

Format: "Brand: keyword subtitle". Play title and App Store name are both capped at 30 characters.

## 1. Hours and pay tracker (working name Shift Pay)

| Name | Chars | What turned up | Confidence it's free |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wagebird: Hours & Pay Tracker** (top pick) | 29 | No app or company named Wagebird. Nearest is "earlybird", an earned-wage-access app on Play (different product, different name). | Fairly high |
| Hourtab: Shift & Pay Tracker | 28 | No app named Hourtab. Similar-sounding: Hourpad (hours/overtime time card for iPhone), plus many "Hourly"/"Hours" apps. | Medium |
| Clockbird: Shift Pay Tracker | 28 | No app named Clockbird. BirdClock (alarm clock app) and "Hourly Bird Chime" exist, different category. | Medium |

Why Wagebird: nothing in either store uses it, it says "wages" at a glance, and it's friendly and easy to say.

Ruled out: Punchly (Punchly Time Tracker already on iOS), Hourbee (HourlyBee is an hourly-jobs
marketplace aimed at the same users), Hourwell (domain parked and the name is tied to a flagged forex
broker), Shiftsy (Shifty, Shiftey, Shiftify, Shyft all exist), Hourlo (HourFlow, Hourlog, Hourli,
Hourlyo), Overtyme (reads as the generic word "overtime"), Shiftcoin/Payhop (crypto and payments
apps).

## 2. Car service and fuel log (working name Fuel & Service Log)

| Name | Chars | What turned up | Confidence it's free |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lugnut: Car Service & Fuel Log** (top pick) | 30 | No app named Lugnut. Some local repair shops use "Lugnut Auto" (Arizona, Colorado), and Lugnut is a Transformers character name (Hasbro). Neither is an app. | Medium-high |
| Wrenchnote: Car Service Log | 27 | No app or company named Wrenchnote. Same-category lookalikes: Wheelnote (car app on Product Hunt), WrenchTime, WrenchNet. | Medium-high |
| Carlore: Fuel & Maintenance | 27 | No app named Carlore. Nearby: CarLo (car locator), Carlover. | Medium |

Why Lugnut: memorable, clearly about cars, and no app in either store uses it. Worth a quick
trademark check because of the Transformers character; a toy trademark normally doesn't cover apps,
but confirm.

Ruled out: Revnote (revnote.app is an OBD-II adapter and app that logs drives, same category),
Odomate (an Android app with that name exists), Revly (Revvy and RevUp car apps), Pitlog (The Pit Log
app, PitSync car manager), Tankful (open-source fuel-economy app of that name, Tankfull IoT on iOS),
Gaugely (AI interview software), Glovebox (used by many car apps as a feature name), Odolog
(Odomtr, Odovo, ODO apps), Torqbook (Torq, Torqy, TorqueLog), Axlenote (AxNote note app).

## 3. Home inventory with warranties and claim report (working name Home Inventory)

| Name | Chars | What turned up | Confidence it's free |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Housetally: Home Inventory** (top pick) | 26 | Nothing at all: no app, company or site uses Housetally. | High |
| Ownery: Home Inventory | 22 | No app named Ownery. Nearby: Ownit (home inventory app), OWND (collection tracker), Owners Inventory (business inventory software). | Medium |
| Roomroll: Home Inventory | 24 | "Room Roll" exists on Google Play (a different kind of app), so it would show up next to yours. | Medium-low |

Why Housetally: the cleanest search result of any name tested, and "tally" says inventory without
being the generic phrase every competitor uses.

Ruled out: ClaimKit (ClaimKit: Home Inventory already on the App Store), HomeLedger (homeowner
documents app on iOS), Hearthly (several chore apps), Proofbox (ProofBox app on iOS, plus "Home Proof
Inventory"), Belongly (on the App Store), Cubbi (Cubby - Home Inventory), Keepsy/Keepbook (Keepory,
Keepstuff, KeepTrack), Stashbook (Stash, StashDog home inventory apps), Stuffwise (StuffKeeper,
Stufflog, StuffID, Stuffinder), Thingbook (Thingsbook).

## Notes

- The subtitle carries the search terms people type ("hours tracker", "pay tracker", "fuel log",
  "car maintenance", "home inventory"), so the brand doesn't have to.
- App Store also has a separate 30-character subtitle field, so the App Store name could be just the
  brand plus a short phrase and the subtitle can hold more keywords.
- Domains were not checked or bought.
